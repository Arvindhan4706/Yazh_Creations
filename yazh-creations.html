About the studio

Made by hand, in small batches

Everything here is made in-house — resin poured and cured a little at a time, crochet stitched row by row. Nothing is mass-produced, so please allow a little time for us to make yours.

Most pieces can be adjusted to fit what you have in mind. If you're not sure what's possible, the easiest way to find out is to ask us on WhatsApp.
