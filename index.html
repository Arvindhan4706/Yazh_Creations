Frequently Asked Questions & Care Guide

Everything you need to know about crafting times, resin maintenance, washing crochet, and shipping.

Resin items require careful multi-layer pouring and a minimum 48 to 72 hours cure time to achieve crystal clarity and scratch resistance. Crochet items take 2 to 4 days depending on intricacy. Standard turnaround time is 4 to 7 business days plus 2 to 4 days transit across India. If you need it for a specific date, let us know on WhatsApp and we will prioritize it!
Yes! We use high-grade, heat-tolerant art resin that safely supports warm tea and coffee cups up to 75°C. To maintain their glossy finish, avoid placing boiling cookware directly from the stove onto them, and hand-wash with lukewarm water and a soft microfibre cloth.
For crochet tote bags, scrunchies, and plush toys, we recommend gentle hand washing in cool water with mild liquid detergent. Reshape the bag while damp and dry flat on a clean towel in the shade. Avoid wringing or harsh machine tumble drying.
Absolutely! We frequently create sentimental keepsake preservation pieces from wedding garlands, graduation bouquets, and anniversary flowers. Contact us as soon as possible after your event so we can guide you on drying or mailing fresh blooms to our studio.
We ship pan-India via express courier with tracked delivery. All resin items are individually wrapped in protective cushioning and nestled inside sturdy gift boxes, ensuring they reach you in pristine, ready-to-gift condition.

Our Philosophy

Slow-Crafted, Heart-Centered Artistry

Yazh Creations was born out of an appreciation for tactile, slow-made art in an era of mass factory production. We believe that everyday objects — the coaster under your morning coffee, the necklace around your collar, or the tote on your shoulder — should hold personal meaning and warmth.

Each resin piece is cured over several days to ensure diamond clarity and durability. Every crochet stitch is counted by hand, creating heirlooms that carry character, love, and story.
